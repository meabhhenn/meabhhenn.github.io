Swing Bay Layout — one continuous line, not separate sets

Two views of the same idea: a single unbroken run of swings you advance through, weaving between them, rather than isolated swing-set islands with grass gaps chopping it up.

Side view the vibe

A repeating arch support every row, each holding one swing at a different point in its arc — reads as one continuous bay when the follow-camera looks down its length, like your reference photo.

Top-down schematic how it plays

Each row (one hop forward) has exactly one swing at a random lane. Empty lanes in that row are always safe — the main strategy is stepping into the gap. If your lane is the one with a swing, you wait for it to be at the back of its arc, then hop through.
